About the Application

Joker Jewels Candy Gems is a colorful casual mobile game developed by Cyber Builder. The application is designed to provide a relaxing, accessible, and enjoyable experience for players.

The project is maintained with a focus on improving stability, visual quality, usability, and the overall user experience through future updates.

Current Application Model

Joker Jewels Candy Gems is currently available without paid access, paid features, or recurring subscription plans. Users are not required to register, log in, or create an account in order to use the application.

The application may include game progress, achievements, selected visual preferences, and an optional local profile for personalization. These elements are stored locally on the user's device.

Advertising Monetization

Joker Jewels Candy Gems uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Advertising helps support ongoing development, maintenance, updates, and improvements without requiring paid access from users.

Information about data processing related to advertising is provided in the Privacy Policy.

Attribution and Campaign Measurement

Joker Jewels Candy Gems uses AppsFlyer for mobile attribution, installation measurement, and advertising campaign performance analysis.

AppsFlyer helps measure how users discover the application and supports campaign analysis connected with the promotion and improvement of the app. Further details are described in the Privacy Policy.

No Purchases or Subscriptions at This Time

The current version of Joker Jewels Candy Gems does not offer in-app purchases, premium features, or subscriptions. If paid features, purchases, or subscriptions are introduced in the future, this page and other relevant public information will be updated clearly.
